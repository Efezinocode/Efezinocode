About me

I'm Uzezi Great Efezino, a Nigerian developer, AI builder and founder of PassSabi AI. My journey into technology has been driven by curiosity, ambition, and a simple belief: you don't have to know everything before you start building.

I started by learning the fundamentals of programming, especially Python, and gradually expanded into HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Git and GitHub. Rather than limiting myself to tutorials, I began turning what I learned into actual projects.

Building calculators and small applications helped me understand programming logic, functions, user input, calculations, interfaces, debugging and problem solving.

I don't want to only use technology.
I want to understand it, build with it, and use it to solve real problems.

As I became more interested in technology, my focus moved beyond simply writing code. I became increasingly interested in AI, software development, product building and entrepreneurship.

I started asking a bigger question: What can I actually build that could make someone's life easier?

That question led to PassSabi AI, my biggest product-building project so far. The idea is to explore how AI can make learning more interactive and accessible for students.

Building PassSabi has pushed me beyond basic programming and into areas such as authentication, databases, APIs, AI integration, responsive web development, Supabase, Vercel, Progressive Web Apps and product design.

One of the biggest lessons I've learned is that creating a product is very different from simply completing a coding tutorial. Things break. Deployments can fail. APIs can return errors. Authentication can become complicated.

Those experiences have made debugging and understanding code just as important to me as writing code.

I'm especially interested in learning how to use AI as a development partner without becoming dependent on blindly copying AI-generated code.

My goal is to understand the code well enough to read it, question it, identify bugs, improve it, and understand why a solution works.

My journey is still ongoing. I'm continuing to grow across Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, modern web development, AI development, software engineering and product development.

Beyond coding, I'm interested in real-world problems, entrepreneurship, hackathons, technology and opportunities to build useful products.

I don't want my projects to simply demonstrate something I learned. I want to keep developing things that can eventually become genuinely useful.

Coming from Nigeria, I see many problems around me that technology could potentially help solve. That motivates me to keep learning, experimenting, debugging and building.

My long-term direction is to become a strong software developer, AI builder and founder capable of turning real problems into useful technology.

I'm still at the beginning of that journey, but I'm taking it seriously — one project, one bug, one lesson and one product at a time.
