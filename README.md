A student-focused AI learning assistant designed to help learners ask questions, understand difficult topics, and prepare for school and external examinations.

- **Live application:** [passsabi-ai.vercel.app](https://passsabi-ai.vercel.app/)
- **Source code:** The actively developed application source is maintained in a private repository; the live demo and public case study are available above.
- **Portfolio case study:** [How I built PassSabi AI](https://uzezigreatefezino.vercel.app/passsabi.html)

### 🛍️ Sabi Demand

An AI-assisted market-demand and business-opportunity research tool for aspiring entrepreneurs and small sellers. It is designed to help people explore what to sell, where to sell it, pricing, competitors, suppliers, and sales channels using budget and location as context.

- **Live application:** [sabi-demand.vercel.app](https://sabi-demand.vercel.app/)
- **Portfolio case study:** [Sabi Demand](https://uzezigreatefezino.vercel.app/sabi-demand.html)

Sabi Demand is a research aid, not a promise of sales or guaranteed profit.
